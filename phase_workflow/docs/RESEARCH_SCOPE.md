# M.Tech Research Scope and Evidence

**Four official phases:** RTL Design; RTL Parsing to Python; AI/LLM Verification; UART Novelty.

**Measured:** initial ten-scenario Hermes coverage 100% of 10 bins; five fresh Hermes trials averaged 98%; balanced-directed achieved the same maximum coverage. Corrected-checker mutation score was 100% for the 14 selected mutants for Hermes and balanced-directed, not universal correctness. Targeted feedback closed one real 90% to 100% gap; matched directed feedback also achieved 100%. UART area/power figures are pre-layout architecture results independently of AI.

**Limitations:** ten-bin coverage, limited fault operators and model trials; external technology library license; institutional formatting and publication-level novelty require separate review. Original historical execution sources and outputs remain preserved.
